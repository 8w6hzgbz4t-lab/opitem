STEAL AN OP ITEM

Buy a powerful collectible, carry it home, and survive the trip. Other players can stop your delivery with powers. Lose a purchased item before reaching your base and you lose the cash paid.

Build your collection, protect your vault, raid open bases, and upgrade for more income and a second floor. Discover 76 collectibles and choose from ten powers, including an aimed Ki Blast, auto-locking Lightning Strike and Spinjitzu.

Controls: hold E / controller X / touch to buy or steal. Click a power or press 1–3 to select. Press F / right trigger / touch Use to activate. Cross your own base's green entrance line to deliver. Settings includes separate music and sound-effect volume and mute controls.

Developer powers are private and cannot be purchased. Robux offers, when configured and enabled, contain only cash, base levels and regular power unlocks. Conveyor collectibles are not sold for Robux.
